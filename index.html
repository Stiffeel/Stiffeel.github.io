石涵玉

name in Chinese — "jade inside a rock"

Born in Lanzhou in 2003, went to Shanghai for my undergraduate studies during COVID, and now pursuing my master’s in the Netherlands — from the highlands to sea level, farther and farther from where I began. 2003 年生于兰州，疫情时代去上海读本科，如今又到了荷兰读硕士，从高原到海平面，越走越远。喜欢语言，喜欢追溯文字、发音的根源；近乎痴迷地旅行，喜欢火车、边境、荒原。

2023
Power Electronics Project 电力电子项目

Programmable DC–DC Power Supply

可编程 DC–DC 电源

Built a TPS40200-based buck converter with a 10–20 V input and regulated 5 V / 2 A output, covering power-stage calculations, component selection, PCB design and system debugging. Added MCU-based monitoring and control on top of the analogue regulation loop to implement programmable current, protection and dual-channel current sharing.

基于 TPS40200 的 10–20 V 输入、5 V / 2 A 输出 Buck 电源，完成从功率级计算、器件选型到 PCB 与整机调试。 在模拟闭环稳压基础上加入 MCU 监测与控制，实现程控电流、保护以及双路并联均流。

调制解调系统

大型课设，完整的调制解调通信链路，将调制、解调与信号处理模块连接为端到端系统。

2024
IEEE CAS Student Design Competition · Winner IEEE 电路与系统协会学生设计竞赛 · 冠军

Multimodal Digital Stethoscope with Intelligent Heart–Lung Sound Separation

支持智能心肺音分离的多模态数字听诊器

Developed a wearable digital stethoscope integrating heart–lung sounds, ECG and body-temperature acquisition, and received First Prize in the IEEE CAS Student Design Competition. Led and contributed to requirements and competitor analysis while integrating the analogue front end, on-chip DSP and BLE; also deployed compressed sensing and the LungHeartAtMe heart–lung sound classification algorithm.

集心肺音、ECG 与体温采集于一体的可穿戴数字听诊器，获得 IEEE CAS 学生设计竞赛一等奖。主导并参与需求与竞品分析，集成模拟前端、片上DSP与BLE等模块； 部署压缩感知与心肺音分类算法（LungHeartAtMe）。

Bachelor Thesis 本科毕业设计

Design & Simulation of Optical Pooling Networks for AI Data Centers

面向 AI 数据中心的光池化网络设计与仿真

My bachelor thesis, “Design and Performance Analysis of an Optical Pooling Network Architecture for Artificial Intelligent Data Centers,” focused on optical interconnects and resource pooling in AI data centers. Starting from the system architecture, I studied network organisation and evaluated performance under different network and resource-allocation configurations.

本科毕业论文《面向人工智能数据中心的光池化网络架构设计与性能分析》关注 AI Data Center 中的光互连与资源池化。 从系统架构出发研究网络组织方式，并对不同网络与资源配置条件下的性能进行分析。

ENHANCE Micro-project ENHANCE 微项目

Gender & Diversity in Science, Technology and Society

科学、技术与社会中的性别与多样性

Selected as one of 30 participants from around 200 applicants for a TU Berlin course held around International Women’s Day; studied humanities and social theory in a wonderfully mixed group, asking how technology is shaped by society — and how it shapes society in return. I also wandered through Berlin in the middle of its Women’s Day celebrations, and still think back very fondly on that week.

2026
Mixed-Signal IC Project 混合信号集成电路项目

Second-Order Noise-Shaping TDC

二阶噪声整形TDC

Designed a second-order noise-shaping TDC in Cadence using a 180 nm process, with two multipath gated-ring oscillators and residue transfer providing second-order shaping of time-quantisation noise. The design achieved 54.9 dB SNDR at OSR 32.

基于Cadence的 180nm 工艺二阶噪声整形 TDC，以双多路径门控环形振荡器和残差传递实现二阶时间量化噪声整形。 设计在 OSR 32 下达到 54.9 dB SNDR。

04China Tours甘肃&上海导游

Currently paused

See China up close — from vibrant cities to vast landscapes.

这么近，那么美。

Guiding, for me, is a way to know more closely the places I come from and the places I pass through, while quietly sharing the curiosity and affection that years of travel have left with me. Shanghai moves with restless energy, its streets dense with everyday life; Gansu opens into vast mountains and rivers, long roads, and layers of history. I offer bespoke English-language tours for international visitors.

做导游，是想借一次次同行，更好地认识自己所来所往之处，也把这些年行走各地积累下来的好奇与热情，安静地分享给远道而来的人。上海有不断向前的速度、街巷里密集而鲜活的日常；甘肃则有辽阔的山河、漫长的道路，以及层层叠叠的历史。为国际游客提供英语定制导览。
