First time in China?

第一次来中国？

China is actually much easier to travel than it may seem from the outside. Still, your first few hours can be a lot: payments, apps, maps, train tickets, reservations, public transport, and the language barrier. You could call all of that part of the adventure — but when your payment stops working or the train you need is suddenly sold out, the charm wears off pretty quickly.

中国其实比想象中容易旅行，但第一次落地时，支付、App、地图、火车票、预约、公共交通和语言障碍， 还是很容易让人抓狂。你当然可以说这些小插曲也是旅行体验的一部分。 但真出问题的时候，实在影响心情。

  • Sort out the boring stuff first 先把麻烦事解决掉 I can help you prepare for payments, connectivity, useful apps, transport and reservations long before you arrive. 找个帮手，在出发前一个月就帮你确认支付、网络、常用 App、交通与预约方式， 免得落地以后才发现寸步难行。
  • Routes that actually make sense 路线首先要走得通 I plan around real distances, opening hours, transport, weather and your own pace — not around how many pins fit into one day. 根据真实距离、开放时间、交通、天气和你的旅行节奏规划， 而不是一天塞进多少个景点。
  • Go beyond the guidebook 攻略之外 What is popular online is not always worth the queue, sometimes not even real. I can help you dodge the worst crowds, find street food and local markets, wander into everyday neighbourhoods, and see a little more of how people actually live. And yes, I also know the best instagram-worthy spots. 网上最热门的不一定最好，甚至不一定是真的；熟悉当地的人可以帮你避开人山人海， 也带你去吃 street food、逛市场、钻进街头巷尾看看普通人的日常。 当然，也知道哪些机位最出片。

More

甘肃周边

Head west from Lanzhou through the Hexi Corridor and across the Gobi, towards the heart of Central Asia — landscapes so vast they can feel almost unreal. Turn south into the Tibetan plateau instead, and the journey becomes quieter, higher, and a little more spiritual.

从兰州向西，穿过河西走廊与戈壁滩，走向中亚腹地， 是近乎不似人间的广袤壮美；向南进入高原藏地， 山川、寺院与漫长的道路，一场人文与灵性之旅。
